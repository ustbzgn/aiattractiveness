Create a premium editorial facial attractiveness analysis report poster for a fictional website called “AI Attractiveness Test”.

VERTICAL FORMAT:
2:3 portrait aspect ratio, approximately 1024x1536.
The entire image is a finished luxury beauty-analysis report poster, shown straight-on with no perspective distortion.
No browser chrome, no phone mockup, no surrounding environment.

OVERALL ART DIRECTION:
A sophisticated combination of a luxury European beauty magazine, premium skincare campaign, facial proportion analysis report, and refined editorial infographic.

Use an elegant warm ivory background, approximately #F7F4EE.
Typography in deep warm charcoal, approximately #282522.
Accent color is muted camel brown / champagne bronze, approximately #B28356.
Borders are extremely thin warm gray-beige, approximately #DDD7CE.
Panels use very pale cream and warm white.
The overall finish is matte, soft, expensive, restrained, refined and editorial.

Avoid futuristic AI aesthetics.
Avoid neon.
Avoid purple or blue gradients.
Avoid glossy glassmorphism.
Avoid cyberpunk interfaces.
Avoid dark UI.
Avoid excessive icons.
Avoid thick borders.
Avoid overly rounded mobile-app cards.

The design should look like a printed luxury beauty assessment sheet photographed perfectly flat.
